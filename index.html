Questions

Is this the official Cyber Essentials?

No. Cyber Essentials is run by IASME for the NCSC, and you apply through a Certification Body. Patch & Padlock isn't linked to either. This kit helps you get ready so you pass first time and only pay the assessment fee.

What changed in April 2026?

The requirements moved to version 3.3 and the "Danzell" question set. MFA must be on for every user of every cloud service that offers it, cloud services can't be left out of scope, and the 14-day update rule now covers vendor-advised configuration fixes too. Read the summary.

Does my small business need it?

You need it if a customer, tender or contract asks for it. Central government contracts handling personal data require it. Even if nobody asks, the five controls stop most common attacks.

Will the kit guarantee a pass?

No one can promise that. The kit makes sure you've checked every area the assessor looks at, and flags the automatic fails first.
